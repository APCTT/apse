About APTG

One starting point for discovering technology information across Asia and the Pacific.

Technology information across the region is distributed among many national, institutional and specialized databases. The Asia-Pacific Tech Gateway (APTG) indexes selected information available through public web pages and available APIs, bringing it together in a common discovery interface.

Users can search across multiple source databases, refine results by country, sector and source, and continue to the original website for complete and up-to-date information.
